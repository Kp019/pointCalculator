Frequently Asked Questions about Game Points Calculation

How does the online game points calculator work?
PointCalc allows you to track scores for any board game, card game, or sport. You initialize players, set custom winning rules (like target score, high/low win condition), and add or subtract points in real-time. The board updates instantly and syncs across all devices.
Can I calculate points for card games like Rummy or Uno?
Yes, PointCalc is fully customizable. You can configure it for card games where a low score wins (like Rummy or Hearts) or high score wins (like Uno, Scrabble, or Bridge). You can easily set positive or negative point increments.
Does the point calculator save game history?
Yes, when you sign up for a free account, all your game history is saved automatically in the cloud. You can resume interrupted games on any device, track player stats over time, and view complete round-by-round score logs.
Can multiple players sync scores on their phones?
Yes, PointCalc features real-time cloud sync. When a host updates the score on one screen, the scoreboard updates instantly for all players on their respective devices.
Is PointCalc free to use?
PointCalc is 100% free online score calculator. You can use it as a guest for a quick game without registration, or create a free account to unlock unlimited games, history, and custom rule presets.
